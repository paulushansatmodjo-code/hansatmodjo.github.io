SUMMER was born from a shared love of music and the belief that the best songs are the ones that stay with you.

The band came together through a combination of friendship, creativity, and a mutual desire to write music that felt genuine. Each member brought a different musical background and perspective, creating a natural chemistry that quickly became the foundation of the band.

Early sessions were less about perfection and more about experimentation. Songs were written, rewritten, recorded, and played again until they felt right. Over time, those sessions developed into a distinctive sound that combines emotional songwriting with energetic instrumentation.

The name SUMMER represents more than a season. It represents a feeling—the freedom of being young, the excitement of discovering something new, warm nights spent with friends, spontaneous adventures, and moments that seem to last forever. That feeling has become a central part of the band's identity. SUMMER makes music designed to capture those moments and turn them into something listeners can carry with them.
