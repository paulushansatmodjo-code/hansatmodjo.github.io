SUMMER is constantly writing, recording, and looking toward what's next. The band is currently working on new material while continuing to develop its live show and connect with listeners in new places. Every new song is another step forward.

Every show is another memory. Every listener becomes another part of the SUMMER story. The band's ambition is simple: keep creating music that feels honest, keep playing shows that people remember, and keep building a community around the songs.
