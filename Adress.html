Live

There's a different kind of energy that exists when a band and an audience are in the same room. SUMMER's live shows are built around that energy.

The setlist combines original songs with dynamic arrangements designed specifically for the stage. Quiet moments build into explosive choruses, intimate songs create space to breathe, and high-energy tracks bring the entire room together. Whether it's a packed venue, an outdoor festival, or a small room filled with longtime fans, SUMMER treats every performance as an opportunity to connect. Because when the lights go down and the first song starts, the music belongs to everyone.
