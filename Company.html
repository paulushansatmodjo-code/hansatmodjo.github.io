SUMMER's sound sits somewhere between modern alternative, indie rock, pop, and nostalgic guitar-driven music. The band's songs move between energetic anthems and more intimate, emotional moments. Catchy melodies sit alongside expressive guitar lines, dynamic drums, atmospheric textures, and lyrics inspired by real experiences.

Rather than trying to fit into a single genre, SUMMER focuses on creating songs that feel authentic to the band. The result is a sound that can feel bright and uplifting one moment, then nostalgic and reflective the next.

At its core, SUMMER's music is about emotion. It's about the excitement of meeting someone new, the feeling of leaving everything behind for a night, friendships that change your life, relationships that don't work out, and memories that you find yourself returning to years later.
